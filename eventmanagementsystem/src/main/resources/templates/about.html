About EventHub

Smart Event Management System — a unified platform for organizers to create, manage, and promote events, and for participants to discover, register, and attend.

We support music concerts, college fests, sports tournaments, professional conferences, and interactive workshops. Our mission is to make event management seamless and accessible to everyone.

Our Vision

To empower communities by bridging the gap between event organizers and participants through technology, supporting the UN Sustainable Development Goals for quality education, economic growth, innovation, sustainable cities, and responsible consumption.

What We Offer

  • Easy event creation and management for organizers
  • Secure ticket booking with QR code-based digital tickets
  • Real-time seat availability and instant registration
  • Feedback and rating system for continuous improvement
  • Role-based dashboards for Admin, Organizer, and Participant
Tech Stack
  • Java 17 + Spring Boot
  • Spring Security
  • Hibernate / JPA
  • MySQL Database
  • Bootstrap 5 + Thymeleaf
  • JavaScript + AJAX
